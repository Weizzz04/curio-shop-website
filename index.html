FAQ

Good to know before you visit.

Why does Curio's stock change so often?

We source cleared warehouse stock and value merchandise, so availability and quantities vary. That changing mix is part of the Curio experience.

Can I check whether something is still available?

Yes. WhatsApp us on 066 214 1618 and we can help with current availability.

Is the website a live product catalogue?

No. The website explains Curio and shows the types of products you may find. Because stock changes regularly, the store and WhatsApp are the best places to check current finds.

Where is the store?

18 Brabazon Rd, Croydon, Kempton Park, 1619. Use the Get Directions button for the live Google Maps listing.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00. We are closed on Saturdays and Sundays.
